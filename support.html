Frequently Asked Questions

What is the goal of Snake MODERN?

The goal is classic and simple: control your snake to eat the food pellets that appear on the screen. Each pellet makes your snake grow longer. The game ends when the snake runs into a wall or its own body. The longer your snake, the higher your score!

How do I control the snake?

Use swipe gestures to control your snake's direction. Swipe up, down, left, or right to change direction. The snake will continue moving in the last direction until you swipe to change it. Quick reflexes are key to avoiding collisions!

Is there a way to pause the game?

Yes! Tap the pause button in the top corner of the screen during gameplay. This will freeze the game until you're ready to continue. Your score and progress are saved when you pause.

🎮 Game Modes

Snake MODERN features multiple difficulty levels. Start with Classic mode and work your way up to Challenge mode for an intense experience!
