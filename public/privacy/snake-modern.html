Snake MODERN — Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

Last updated: January 2025

Kaspar Digital ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Snake MODERN ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Snake MODERN is designed with privacy in mind. The App:

  • Does NOT collect personal information such as your name, email, or location
  • Does NOT transmit any data to our servers during normal gameplay
  • Stores locally only your high score and game preferences on your device

2. Local Data Storage

The following data is stored solely on your device and is never shared:

  • Your highest score
  • Game settings and preferences (e.g., sound on/off)
  • Unlock progress within the app

This data remains on your iPhone or iPad and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services

Snake MODERN may use analytics or crash reporting services provided by Apple (e.g., App Store Connect analytics). These services collect anonymous usage data. No personally identifiable information is shared.

4. Children's Privacy

Snake MODERN does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is suitable for all ages and does not require any personal data to function.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
